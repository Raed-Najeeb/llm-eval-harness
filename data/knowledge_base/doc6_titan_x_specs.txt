Nova Robotics Titan-X Heavy Lifter — Product Specification

The Titan-X is designed for heavy manufacturing environments with a peak
towing capacity of 1,200 kg. It utilizes dual lithium-ion power packs providing
up to 14 hours of continuous deployment. The core chassis has an IP65 water
and dust resistance rating. Operational sound output peaks at 68 dB during
maximum load transport.
